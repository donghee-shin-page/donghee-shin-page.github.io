👋 About Me

Hello! I'm Dong-Hee Shin, a Ph.D. student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Korea University, where I work under the supervision of Professor Tae-Eui Kam.

My main research interests are in molecular discovery, particularly in new drug discovery and novel material design. On the AI side, I focus on reinforcement learning and the development of more effective optimization algorithms to support scientific innovation.
